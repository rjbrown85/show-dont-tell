# ✏️ Show, Don't Tell

A Grade 5 writing tool. Students pick a feeling and get similes and metaphors that **show** it instead of telling it.

- 300 figurative-language ideas across 40 feelings in 9 feeling families
- 200 common similes and metaphors, each with a kid-friendly meaning, for the 30 feelings on the classroom Show, Don't Tell chart
- 100 more for 10 new feelings (calm, grateful, hopeful, loved, relieved, homesick, annoyed, jealous, guilty, determined): similes, metaphors, 🗣️ personification, 💥 hyperbole, 💡 idioms, and 🌏 27 real Swedish, Chinese, and Korean idioms shown in their original language
- Swedish, Simplified Chinese, and Korean support for every phrase and meaning
- A grand piano chord or short piece that plays when a feeling opens, and a soft piano tap on other buttons (🔊 / 🔇 toggle)
- Filters by type of figurative language, a 🎲 Surprise me button, and a 📋 copy button on every card

The full list is in [BANK.md](BANK.md).

No build step and no outside libraries or fonts: everything, including the piano, is inside `index.html`, so it loads well in Shanghai and works offline.

See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for the piano samples and sources.
