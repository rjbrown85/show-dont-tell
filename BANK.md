# Show, Don't Tell: Simile and Metaphor Bank

300 figurative-language ideas for Grade 5 writers: 200 common similes and metaphors for the 30 feelings on the classroom Show, Don't Tell chart, plus 100 more (similes, metaphors, personification, hyperbole, idioms, and Swedish, Chinese, and Korean idioms) for 10 more feelings. Grouped into 9 feeling families.
