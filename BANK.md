# Show, Don't Tell: Simile and Metaphor Bank

300 show-don't-tell ideas for Grade 5 writers: 200 common similes and metaphors for the 30 feelings on the classroom Show, Don't Tell chart, plus 100 sensory lines (see, hear, smell, taste, touch, inside my body, action, dialogue, thoughts) for 10 more feelings. Grouped into 9 feeling families.
